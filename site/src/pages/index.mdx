description: "PACE, the Portable Account Core for Ethereum, is an industry effort to agree on the part of Ethereum's new account that every wallet builds the same way: a spec for EIP-8130 keys and sessions, a threshold authenticator, ERC-7715 and ERC-7710 app permissions, EIP-7906 assertions and ERC-7730 clear signing metadata, plus a few shared contracts. Wallets add everything else on top. Stewarded by Ethlabs."

## Move to post-quantum keys

Quantum computers could break ECDSA, the signature behind every Ethereum key today. In EIP-8130 a contract verifies each key’s signature, so a post-quantum key is just another key, and you keep your address and funds.

</div>

## Approve the result you expect

Getting drained is the biggest risk of holding your own keys. A co-signer can refuse a bad transaction before it counts, and the chain reverts any result you didn’t approve.

</div>

## Read every transaction on your hardware wallet

A hacked web page can show one thing and ask you to sign another, which is how Bybit lost about $1.5 billion in 2025. PACE includes clear signing metadata for every operation, so the hardware wallet decodes the transaction itself and a hacked screen can’t fool you.

</div>
